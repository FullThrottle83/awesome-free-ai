# Creative AI catalog update (October 2026)

Baseline: 139 entries on `main` before this batch.

## Editorial decisions

- Updated existing image, music, and video descriptions with dated first-party sources.
- Removed Udio from the hosted free-tools directory: [official notice](https://help.udio.com/en/articles/12683565-changes-associated-with-the-universal-music-group-umg-partnership) says audio/video/stem downloads are disabled. Its free listening value may still be reconsidered.
- Moved ElevateAI from Text & Language into Infrastructure & APIs. **Current exact free quota remains unverified**, so it is marked for follow-up.
- Kept whisper.cpp unchanged.
- Added 15 selected candidates from Gemini's 22 discoveries, favouring distinctive tools, self-hostable software and clearly constrained free access.

## Deferred candidates

OpenVINO AI Plugins for Audacity, ElevenLabs, Kdenlive, Video2X, Subtitle Edit, Harper, and DeepL Translator / Write were not added in this pass. These need either sharper AI-specific value, further pricing/license review, or a clearer place in the taxonomy.

## Evidence standards and limitations

- Links in README point to primary vendor documentation, model cards or repositories.
- Free software does not imply free hardware, API calls, or universally commercial model weights.
- Source checked dates derive from the October 19 Gemini research with independent spot checks on October 20.
- No hands-on product testing is claimed.
- The remaining older catalog outside this batch is still not revalidated.
